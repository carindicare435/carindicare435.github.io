Why a regular check pays off

Save money on fuel

Correct tyre pressure and wheel alignment, clean air filters and a healthy engine all help a car use fuel efficiently.

Make the car safe

Worn tyres, a weak battery and tired brakes are easier and cheaper to deal with when they are caught early.

Budget your repairs and maintenance

Repairs planned before they become emergencies. Your report guides you on what’s urgent and what can wait. Like rotating your tyres so they last longer before replacement.

Straight to the fix

Your report names the problem. Get a quote from your garage and the repair booked, with no waiting at the garage or leaving the car.

What the technician checks

Six areas, each one chosen because it's the first thing to show wear, and the first thing you notice on the road.

Tyres

Pressure, tread depth, cuts and uneven wear.

Battery

A load test that shows whether the battery can still start the car, with cold cranking amps and age on the report.

Brakes

Pad wear seen through the wheel gap, and a short drive to listen for noise from the steering and suspension.

Fluids

Engine oil, coolant, brake fluid and washer levels, checked for level and colour.

Filters

Air filter, checked where readily accessible, so the engine breathes clean air.

Wipers and washers

Blade condition and washer spray, so visibility stays clear.

Questions

Something missing? Call or WhatsApp us and we'll answer it.

What exactly does the check cover?

Each package lists its checks above. Your report then shows every item with its result, and anything we did not check.

Do you repair the car or sell parts?

No. We check the car and write the report. If something needs fixing, you choose the workshop. We don't sell parts and we don't quote for repairs.

Can I visit a workshop after the check?

Yes, and we suggest it when the report flags something. Take the report with you so the workshop can see what we found.

Which gated communities do you cover?

Communities where the residents' association has agreed to our visits. Send us your community name and we'll tell you whether we can come.

What if I'm not at home?

Leave the keys with the security desk or a named person, or be there for the check. The report comes to your WhatsApp either way.

Can you check an electric car?

Yes, with the EV Visual Check add-on. It covers the visible parts. It does not test the high-voltage battery, and our technicians don't do EV diagnostics. If the car needs an EV specialist, we'll tell you.

How do I pay?

After you book, we send your booking details and how to pay on WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Send us a message on WhatsApp to cancel or reschedule. It's free until the day before your booking. Cancelling or rescheduling on the day costs 10% of the booking.
